# The DeepLore Bar

The DeepLore bar is the row of buttons just above SillyTavern's message box. It holds DeepLore's menu, the side-panel button, your favorite Quick Replies, and a few special send buttons.

You can switch it off in **Settings › Interface › The bar › Show the DeepLore bar**.

---

## The buttons (PC)

From left to right:

- **📖 DeepLore menu**: Open (or Close) the side panel, **Open the board**, **Write a new entry…**, **Move an entry…**, and **Setup and settings**.
- **Side-panel button**: opens the [[Drawer|side panel]]; press it again to close it. It also carries Mero's signal (see below).
- **Your starred Quick Replies**: one button each. A star belongs to its set, so it only shows in chats where that set is on.
- **All quick replies**: the sets SillyTavern has on here: global ones, this chat's and this character's (only those set to visible). Press ☆ next to one to put it on the bar (★ takes it off again). With no set on here, it says so.
- **My notes**: your own notes, never sent to the AI (switch it off in Settings › Interface › The bar). See [[My Notes]].
- **Re-evaluate and swipe**: undo what the newest reply changed on the board, ask again, and write a new swipe.
- **Simple send**: post what you typed as your message, without asking for a reply.
- **Keep as a draft**: put what you typed aside. Press it again to put it back. The draft follows you to other devices. If the box already has other text, DeepLore asks before it swaps the two, so nothing you typed is lost.

While DeepLore is choosing lore, its working line shows on the bar too.

## On a phone

The bar has five buttons: 📖, the side-panel button, **Quick replies**, **My notes**, and **More** (re-evaluate, simple send, draft). Quick replies and More take over the bar's row when you tap them; **‹** goes back. Pull the handle up for more room.

---

## Mero's signal

The side-panel button tells you when Mero ☆ has something for you:

- **Panel not locked:** a small dot appears on the button while Mero has requests. It turns amber (and wiggles now and then) while a reply waits for your answer. Pressing the button then opens straight to her requests.
- **Panel locked open (PC):** the button becomes an envelope. It takes you to Mero's requests, and pressed again, back to where the panel was.

When a reply is waiting for you, the bar also shows **⏸ Waiting for your answer**. Tap it to open the requests.

---

## Moving and writing entries

- **Write a new entry…** opens the board's entries window on its own. The new entry is filed where **Settings › Story & memory › New entries** says, and Mero can draft it.
- **Move an entry…**: pick the entry (newest first, or search), then pick its new folder.

---

## The DeepLore button on each reply

Every message also gets one DeepLore button (📖), next to SillyTavern's own buttons. It opens a small menu:

- **Pitch from here**: ask [[Mero]] for story ideas starting at this message.
- **Why these entries**: which lore went out with this reply, and why.
- **What this changed**: the board changes this reply made (opens Story › What changed).
- **Branch here as a new run**: SillyTavern's branch; the new branch then offers to become a new run.
- **Re-evaluate and swipe**: only on the newest reply.
